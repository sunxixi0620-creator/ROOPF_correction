# Primary-source reading map, 2026-09-30

Targeted reading for ROOPF; not an exhaustive systematic review. “Sections” means
the named parts were inspected, not a claim of full-paper reproduction. New arXiv
preprints are hypothesis-generating evidence, not established guarantees. No
third-party AI summaries are used as technical evidence.

|Source|Status / inspected scope|Role and limit for this project|
|---|---|---|
|[Frazier — A Tutorial on Bayesian Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.02811)|2018 tutorial; abstract/scope|GP, acquisition, evaluation loop; old dimensionality guidance is not a hard present-day limit.|
|[Wang et al. — Pre-trained Gaussian Processes for Bayesian Optimization](https://jmlr.org/papers/v25/23-0269.html)|JMLR 2024; PDF sections on assumptions, EKL/NLL and regret limitations|Source distributions and posterior conditioning; theoretical assumptions do not automatically describe ROOPF tasks.|
|[Feurer et al. — Practical Transfer Learning for Bayesian Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.02219)|2018 initial / 2022 v4; HTML ranking weights and GP ensemble/variance sections|RGPE as transfer baseline. Earlier title was Scalable Meta-Learning for Bayesian Optimization; use version-specific naming.|
|[Wistuba & Grabocka — Few-Shot Bayesian Optimization with Deep Kernel Surrogates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.07667)|ICLR 2021; abstract and publication metadata|Deep-kernel few-shot adaptation; no implementation reproduced here.|
|[Müller et al. — PFNs4BO](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/muller23a.html)|ICML 2023; official abstract|Posterior predictive learning from sampled priors; pretrained checkpoint compatibility not established.|
|[Pan et al. — MALIBO](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/pan24b.html)|ICML 2024; [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.03565v3) methodology and adaptation/ablation sections|Task uncertainty, utility learning and residual adaptation; not evidence for the old ROOPF residual.|
|[Chang et al. — Amortized Probabilistic Conditioning for Optimization, Simulation and Inference](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/chang25a.html)|AISTATS 2025; official abstract|Explicit latent conditioning; architecture inspiration only.|
|[Hvarfner et al. — Vanilla Bayesian Optimization Performs Great in High Dimensions](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/hvarfner24a.html)|ICML 2024; official abstract|Dimension-aware lengthscale prior and strong online baseline; fixed sqrt(d) lengthscale is not a replication.|
|[Ament et al. — Unexpected Improvements to Expected Improvement](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/419f72cbd568ad62183f8132a3605a2a-Abstract-Conference.html)|NeurIPS 2023; official abstract|Numerically stable acquisition optimization; audit symptoms before attributing failure to EI underflow.|
|[Eriksson et al. — Scalable Global Optimization via Local Bayesian Optimization](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/file/6c990b7aca7bc7058f5e98ea909e924b-Paper.pdf)|NeurIPS 2019; PDF abstract/method outline|TuRBO, local models/trust regions; not equivalent to clamped Gaussian candidates.|
|[Eriksson & Jankowiak — High-dimensional BO with Sparse Axis-Aligned Subspaces](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v161/eriksson21a.html)|UAI 2021; official abstract|SAASBO; explicit sparsity/alignment assumptions.|
|[Jiang et al. — Efficient Nonmyopic BO via One-Shot Multi-Step Trees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15779)|NeurIPS 2020; abstract|Decision horizon differs from greedy improvement; not a reason to restart noisy branch-label training.|
|[Huang et al. — Amortized Bayesian Experimental Design for Decision-Making](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/c59f05d7ab3638b138cc61f32e1a7cd1-Abstract-Conference.html)|NeurIPS 2024; official abstract|Experimental information should serve downstream decisions; implementation not inspected.|
|[Kandasamy et al. — Multi-fidelity BO with Continuous Approximations](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/kandasamy17a.html)|ICML 2017; official abstract|BOCA, real fidelity/cost relations; unrelated source tasks are not automatically low fidelity.|
|[Falkner et al. — BOHB](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/falkner18a.html)|ICML 2018; official abstract|Budget allocation and optimization; applicable when evaluation fidelity is controllable.|
|[Turner et al. — Analysis of the Black-Box Optimization Challenge 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v133/turner21a.html)|PMLR 2021; official abstract|Strong practical BO comparisons; implementation is part of the baseline.|
|[HPO-B](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06257) and [official repository](https://github.com/machinelearningnuremberg/HPO-B)|NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks 2021; abstract and README protocol/data/API|v3 train/validation/test, tabular vs surrogate modes, objective direction. No target labels downloaded in this review.|
|[BoTorch Getting Started](https://botorch.org/docs/getting_started)|Official documentation, page states v0.18.1; full short example inspected|GP fitting, LogEI and multi-start acquisition optimization. Local environment currently has no botorch package; no installation/change made.|
|[Hvarfner et al. — Pitfalls and Remedies for Multi-Task Bayesian Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09073)|**2026-07-10 preprint**, v1; HTML introduction, model, proposed pitfalls/remedies|Transfer can fail even under apparent task relation; neither theorem verification nor direct ROOPF diagnosis.|
|[Lee et al. — Transfer Learning Architectures for Scalable Multi-Fidelity BO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23404)|**2026-07-26 preprint**, v1; abstract, study setup and results discussion|Matched acquisition comparisons and task-dependent benefits; do not universalize authors' claims about uncertainty.|
|[Tfaily et al. — Transfer Learning in BO for Aircraft Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28999)|**2026-03-30 preprint**; abstract only|Example of application-specific relationships; not evidence our UAV proxy has the same structure.|

Searches also covered neural surrogates, batch BO and related 2026 reviews. They
are not used to make unverified model-performance claims. Publication recency
alone is not a reason to adopt a method. The main recommendation rests on the
project's measurements and established transfer/BO mechanisms; the 2026 papers
provide additional questions to check.
